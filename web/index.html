What has and has not been verified

  • Export format: DST only. The file is checked by the software (structure, stitch limits, an independent reader) before you can download it. Other formats (PES, JEF, EXP) are not available yet.
  • Machine validation: none yet. A file that downloads without warnings is not proof that your machine will stitch it well. Always stitch a sample on the real fabric first.
  • Fabric presets: experimental starting values. Change them if your sample shows gaps, puckering or thread breaks.
  • Stitch time: an estimate from an assumed speed (700 stitches per minute) plus fixed time for colour changes and trims. Your machine will differ.
  • Trims and jumps: the DST format has no trim command. The number of "trim jumps" is a guess that must be confirmed on your machine (see the trim test in the test pack).
  • Importing artwork: SVG, PNG and JPG. PDF is not supported - export your artwork as SVG, PNG or JPG.

TrueType / OpenType lettering

    
Upload a font you are licensed to embroider with (for Telugu e.g. Noto Sans Telugu, Ramabhadra; Devanagari: Noto Sans Devanagari). Complex scripts are shaped with HarfBuzz (conjuncts, vowel signs). No font = DejaVu Sans (Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Arabic).
Caution: Telugu and Hindi lettering has not been verified with real fonts yet - check the preview carefully, ask a native reader to approve it, and stitch a sample before use. Small letters (under ~8 mm) may not be legible in embroidery.
